Data Engineer — Meridian Analytics (Bengaluru, hybrid)

About us
Meridian Analytics builds the reporting layer for mid-market retailers across
India and South-East Asia. We move a lot of data, and our customers make
buying decisions on the back of it, so correctness matters more here than
almost anywhere else.

The role
You will own ingestion and transformation for a set of customer accounts. That
means building the pipelines that bring raw sales, inventory and supplier data
in, modelling it into something analysts can query without asking us questions,
and keeping it all running. You will work closely with two analytics engineers
and report to the Head of Data.

What you will do
- Build and maintain batch pipelines in Airflow, from source system to warehouse
- Model data in dbt so downstream tables are documented, tested and trusted
- Write performant SQL against large tables and know why a query is slow
- Own data quality: freshness checks, row-count reconciliation, alerting
- Handle streaming ingestion through Kafka for our near-real-time accounts
- Tune Spark jobs when a batch outgrows a single machine

What we need
- 3+ years in a data engineering role
- Strong SQL, including window functions and query plan reading
- Production Python, particularly for data work — Pandas, or PySpark
- Hands-on Airflow, or another orchestrator you can argue about
- Dimensional modelling: facts, dimensions, slowly changing dimensions
- Experience with a cloud warehouse — Snowflake, BigQuery or Redshift
- Comfort on AWS: S3, IAM, and enough networking to unblock yourself

Nice to have
- dbt in production
- Kafka or another streaming platform
- Terraform, or any infrastructure-as-code
- Experience in retail or e-commerce data

What we offer
Competitive salary, health cover for you and your dependants, a hardware budget,
and three days a week in our Indiranagar office. We run a four-week onboarding
where you ship a small pipeline end to end in your first fortnight.
